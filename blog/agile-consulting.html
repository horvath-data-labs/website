Companies dread system conversions. They are seen by office teams around the world as the ultimate root canal of data management. This fear is not without merit—during a traditional conversion, both clients and vendors suffer when fundamental implementation friction goes unaddressed.

The High Friction of Traditional Conversions

  • Wasted SME Capacity: Subject matter experts (SMEs) waste valuable time catching basic errors like blank fields or text values in numeric columns rather than validating core business logic.
  • Fragile "Big Bang" Testing: Vendor-side developers face weeks of siloed pipeline builds culminating in a brittle testing phase where minor uncaught errors erode team trust.
  • Late Schema & Mapping Overhauls: Single misunderstandings in early mapping surface late, forcing massive, costly rewrites across the transformation codebase.
  • Brittle & Unversioned Scripts: Mapping logic gets lost in static spreadsheets or unversioned pipeline scripts, creating a cycle where fixing one bug breaks another key functional area.

This creates a destructive cycle: SME confidence erodes, developer frustration spikes as targets continuously move, and executives watch critical business resources stay tied up month after month with no usable system in sight.
