The Challenge with Internal Data & Off-the-Shelf Tools

  • Isolated SaaS Reporting: Your off-the-shelf SaaS tools all have clean, built-in reporting dashboards and may allow natural language querying for isolated data.
  • No Unified Operational Layer: These tools don't talk to each other, and there is no out-of-the-box reporting layer for the custom database where your core operational data actually lives.
  • Manual & Inconsistent Querying: Teams rely on custom SQL queries, endless spreadsheet exports, or filtering through rigid dropdowns—leading to department heads walking into meetings with conflicting numbers.

Granting AI raw access to an ungoverned data setup only magnifies the chaos, resulting in wild hallucinations and risky business decisions.
